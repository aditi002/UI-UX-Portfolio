Hi, I'm Aditi.

I'm a UI/UX designer at Cubit Incorporated in Kathmandu, designing web and mobile products, many of them for clients in Australia.

I studied computing before I moved into design, so I like knowing how things get built. Most of my days are spent with product managers and developers, turning long requirement lists into flows, components and screens a team can ship. The work I enjoy most is the unglamorous kind: the forty-field form, the dashboard nobody reads, the status that only one person understands. Making those clear changes someone's whole working day. I'm now doing an Executive MBA alongside, because good design also has to make sense for the business.
